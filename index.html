About Dr Abdul Basit

Built on the farm, not just for it.

Dr Abdul Basit is the Managing Director of JS Feeds & Fodders in Hasilpur, and a practicing veterinary doctor with a background in livestock management. What began as farm-level consultancy for dairy and goat owners grew into a dedicated feed and fodder supply business.

The JS Silage Wanda Factory now produces and supplies silage, hay, wanda (compound feed) and mineral mixtures formulated for the specific needs of dairy cattle and goats — from milk yield and weight gain to breeding and growth.
